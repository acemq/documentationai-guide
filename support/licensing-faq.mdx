## Cost and sizing

### How is licensing priced?

Per CPU core, across **all** environments — production and non-production. See [How Licensing Is Counted](/access/core-counting).

### Can we license production only and leave dev/test unlicensed?

**No.** Broadcom's licensing requirements mandate that all pre-production and production environments are licensed. Production-only scoping is not an available option.

### Do you need vCPU or physical core counts?

AceMQ asks for both the total count **and** whether they're physical or virtualized — the two produce different quotes. Always state which you're supplying. See [How Licensing Is Counted](/access/core-counting).

### What's the minimum core commitment?

A minimum applies, and the figure depends on product, region, and purchasing channel. Confirm the one that applies to you with AceMQ before budgeting — see [How Licensing Is Counted](/access/core-counting).

### Can you license us in our region?

AceMQ's selling authorization varies by region and by the purchasing entity's location. Raise this early if your deploying and purchasing entities are in different countries.

### What do you need from us to produce a quote?

Total core count across all environments, whether those are physical or virtual, your RabbitMQ version, and a rough picture of the deployment.

## Terms and billing

### What term lengths are available?

1-year and 3-year. Multi-year is billed annually across the term, not upfront. See [Terms, Billing & Renewals](/access/terms-and-renewals).

### Is my invoice for licensing or for services?

They're billed separately by design. See [Terms, Billing & Renewals](/access/terms-and-renewals).

### How does renewal work?

AceMQ confirms whether your core count changed, then produces a renewal quote. Start early if your approvals take time. See [Terms, Billing & Renewals](/access/terms-and-renewals).

## What the license covers

### Do I need a license key?

No. The license is embedded in the commercial build you pull from `acemq.jfrog.io`. If you pulled it from AceMQ, you're licensed. See [Licensing](/access/licensing).

### Which package formats does my license cover?

All of them — OCI, RPM, OVA, Kubernetes, and Open Source. See [What's Included](/start-here/whats-included).

### Does my support cover our existing Bitnami / open-source deployment?

**Yes.** AceMQ support covers all RabbitMQ deployments — Bitnami, open-source, or Tanzu — under your SLA. Licensing adds LTS support and access to newer commercial builds; it doesn't gate whether AceMQ will help with what you're already running.

### What does licensing actually get me over the community build?

Three things: access to the LTS-supported stream and newer commercial builds (the free community ceiling is 3.13.7), the commercial-only plugins, and AceMQ's commercial support SLA. See [What Is Tanzu?](/start-here/what-is-tanzu).

### Which versions am I entitled to?

Once licensed, you have access to commercial downloads for every supported RabbitMQ version — you can upgrade whenever you choose. See [Version Support Matrix](/start-here/version-support-matrix).

### Is Windows covered?

AceMQ support covers Windows deployments, but **there is no licensed Tanzu package for Windows** — only the Open Source build. See [Open Source RabbitMQ](/packages/open-source).

## Getting your software

### I'm licensed — how do I actually download anything?

You'll receive a JFrog access email and a separate Jira service desk invite. See [Onboarding](/access/onboarding).

### I logged into JFrog but can't download anything

Look under **Artifacts**, not Packages — see [Navigating JFrog](/access/navigating-jfrog). This is the single most common first-login stumble.

### How do I add more people to our access?

Every person needs to be listed on the onboarding submission to receive their own invite. To add someone afterwards, contact AceMQ — see [Getting Help](/support/getting-help). Any email address can be added; there's no domain restriction or seat limit.

### How long after ordering until we have access?

JFrog provisioning is a manual step on AceMQ's side, triggered by your onboarding form submission. Submit the form promptly after your order completes — that's the gate. See [Onboarding](/access/onboarding).

## Security and compliance

### Do you provide CVE information for our build?

Ask AceMQ support for a CVE summary against your specific running version — see [Getting Help](/support/getting-help). This is a common compliance-review request.

### Is FIPS supported?

Yes, in specific artifacts. See [FIPS](/support/fips).

### What are your support response times?

A standard 12-hour response on product-related issues is included with every license, with faster response available under an add-on incident-management contract. See [Getting Help](/support/getting-help).
