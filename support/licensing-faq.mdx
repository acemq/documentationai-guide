## The license itself

### Do I need a license key?

No. The license is embedded in the commercial build you pull from `acemq.jfrog.io`. See [Licensing Overview](/access/licensing).

### Which package formats does my license cover?

All of them — OCI, RPM, OVA, Kubernetes, and Open Source. See [What's Included](/start-here/whats-included).

### Which versions am I entitled to?

Once licensed, you have access to commercial downloads for every supported RabbitMQ version — upgrade whenever you choose. See [Version Support Matrix](/start-here/version-support-matrix).

### What does licensing get me over the community build?

Access to the LTS-supported stream and newer builds (the free community ceiling is 3.13.7), the commercial-only plugins, jump upgrades, and AceMQ's support SLA. See [What Is Tanzu?](/start-here/what-is-tanzu).

### What if our usage has grown beyond what we contracted?

Contact your AceMQ account owner. Nothing technically blocks you, but your entitlement should reflect what you're running — see [Licensing Overview](/access/licensing).

## Support scope

### Does my support cover our existing Bitnami / open-source deployment?

**Yes.** AceMQ support covers all RabbitMQ deployments — Bitnami, open-source, or Tanzu — under your agreement. Licensing adds LTS support and newer commercial builds; it doesn't gate whether AceMQ will help with what you're already running.

### What are your support response times?

A standard 12-hour response on product-related issues is included, with faster response available under an add-on incident-management contract. See [Getting Help](/support/getting-help).

## Platforms

### Is Windows supported?

Yes — Windows builds are available from the AceMQ repository, and AceMQ supports Windows RabbitMQ deployments. See [Open Source RabbitMQ](/packages/open-source) for where to find them.

### Which Linux distributions does the RPM support?

RHEL 8 and 9 and EL8/EL9-compatible distributions. See [RPM → Supported Platforms](/docs/rpm/4.3/platforms).

### Is FIPS supported?

Yes, in specific artifacts — OVA, OCI, and the Kubernetes Helm chart. The RPM requires host-level FIPS configuration. See [FIPS](/support/fips).

### Can I run this air-gapped?

Yes. See [Air-Gapped Environments](/packages/air-gapped).

## Getting your software

### I'm licensed — how do I actually download anything?

You'll receive a JFrog access email and a separate Jira service desk invite. See [Onboarding](/access/onboarding).

### I logged into JFrog but can't download anything

Look under **Artifacts**, not Packages — see [Navigating JFrog](/access/navigating-jfrog). This is the most common first-login stumble.

### I get a permissions error when pulling

Usually a token-scope problem rather than an entitlement problem. See [Generate a Token](/access/generate-token) and [Access Troubleshooting](/support/access-troubleshooting).

### How do I add more people to our access?

Everyone who needs access should be listed on the onboarding submission to receive their own invite. To add someone afterwards, contact AceMQ — see [Getting Help](/support/getting-help). Any email address can be added; there's no domain restriction or seat limit.

## Security and compliance

### Do you provide CVE information for our build?

Ask AceMQ support for a CVE summary against your specific running version — see [Getting Help](/support/getting-help). This is a common compliance-review request.
