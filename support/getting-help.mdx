## Opening a ticket

Support requests go through the **Jira service desk**, a separate invite from your JFrog access (see [Onboarding](/access/onboarding)). If you didn't receive your Jira invite, contact AceMQ to have it resent.

## What to include

To get a faster resolution, include:

- The exact package/version you're running (format, tag or filename)
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
- The exact error text — see [Access Troubleshooting](/support/access-troubleshooting) first in case it's already documented

## Response times

- **Standard:** 12-hour response on product-related issues, included with every license.
- **Emergency escalation and incident management:** available as an add-on contract, at a discount for licensed customers, for faster production-incident response.

## Scope

AceMQ support covers all RabbitMQ deployments — Bitnami/open-source or Tanzu — not just licensed builds. For licensing, access, or registry questions specifically, this ticket path is also the right place to ask; AceMQ will loop in Broadcom under your subscription if needed.
