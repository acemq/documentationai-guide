---
title: Creating a Queue in AceMQ RabbitMQ
description: Quickly create a durable queue using AceMQ console.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login to Dashboard">
    Navigate to `https://acemq.com/dashboard` and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Queues">
    Click **Queues**, then **Add Queue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Enter queue name, set **Durable** to true, leave others default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Queue">
    Click **Create**. Your queue appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>
