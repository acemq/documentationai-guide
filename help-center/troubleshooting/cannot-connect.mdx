---
title: Cannot Connect to RabbitMQ Cluster
description: Quick fixes if you can't connect to your AceMQ Tanzu RabbitMQ deployment.
---

## Symptoms
- Connection refused
- Timeout errors in client logs

## Common Causes
- Incorrect hostname or port
- Firewall blocking port 5672

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ping the server">
    ````bash
    ping your-rabbitmq-host.acemq.com
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test port access">
    ````bash
    telnet your-rabbitmq-host.acemq.com 5672
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check credentials">
    Ensure username/password are correct in your client config.
  </Step>
</Steps>
