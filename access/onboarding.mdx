---
title: Onboarding
description: What happens after your PO is finalized — the onboarding form, JFrog provisioning, and what arrives in your inbox.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the onboarding form" icon="clipboard">

  Once your PO is finalized, go to **[onboarding.acemq.com](https://onboarding.acemq.com)** and select **License and Support Onboarding**. List every user who will need JFrog and support-portal access — access is provisioned per person, not per company.

  </Step>
  <Step title="AceMQ provisions your JFrog account" icon="settings">

  This is a manual step on AceMQ's side, not instant. A group is created for your company and repository permissions are attached. There's no published SLA — expect it shortly after submission, not immediately.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for two separate emails" icon="mail">

  You'll receive **two different invitations**, for two different systems:

  - A **JFrog access email** ("Your AceMQ RabbitMQ Artifact Access is Ready") with a temporary password for `acemq.jfrog.io`
  - A separate **Jira service desk invite** for support tickets

  Every user listed on the onboarding form gets their own invite. If someone doesn't see theirs, check spam/updates folders before assuming it wasn't sent — invites are commonly missed this way.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and set your password" icon="key">

  Continue to [Your JFrog Account](/access/jfrog-account) for first login and token setup.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

**Any user with an email address can be added to JFrog** — there's no domain restriction or seat limit. If you need a shared "service account" for CI/CD, invite a shared team mailbox the same way you'd invite an individual.

</Callout>
