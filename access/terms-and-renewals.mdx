## Terms

AceMQ offers **1-year and 3-year** license terms. Multi-year terms are billed **annually across the term**, not upfront — a 3-year agreement is invoiced once per year at the agreed annual rate.

If your organization can't commit to a multi-year agreement, a 1-year term is available.

## Licensing and professional services are billed separately

<Callout kind="info">

Commercial licensing and any professional services engagement (upgrades, migrations, assessments) are **intentionally invoiced separately**. A licensing proposal covers licensing only; services work is quoted and invoiced on its own. If you receive an invoice and aren't sure which it covers, ask — the separation is deliberate so each can be approved on its own track.

</Callout>

## Renewals

Your license term runs to an anniversary date. Ahead of it, AceMQ will typically confirm:

- Whether your **core count has changed** since the last term — see [How Licensing Is Counted](/access/core-counting)
- Whether the rate is unchanged
- Your preferred term length for the renewal

To start a renewal yourself, contact your account team or open a ticket — see [Getting Help](/support/getting-help). Bring your current core counts across all environments; that's the information needed to produce a renewal quote.

<Callout kind="alert">

**AceMQ Insight**

Start renewal conversations well before the anniversary date if your procurement process involves multiple approvals. AceMQ has seen terms lapse purely because internal approval cycles ran longer than expected — which is avoidable with lead time.

</Callout>

## If your term lapses during renewal approvals

If your renewal is genuinely in progress but internal approvals haven't completed by the anniversary date, contact AceMQ rather than assuming coverage continues automatically. AceMQ handles these case by case; get the specifics for your situation in writing from your account contact.

## Contracting paperwork

For MSA, order form, SOW, or DPA requirements, work with your AceMQ account contact. Legal and contracting documents are handled per-agreement rather than published here.

## Budget planning

If you need pricing for a future budget cycle, ask early — quotes depend on core counts and term length, and lead times can be affected by Broadcom's own quarter-end cycles.
