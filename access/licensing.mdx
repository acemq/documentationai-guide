## There is no license key

There is no license key file, environment variable, or configuration setting to apply. **The license is embedded in the commercial build itself.** If you pulled it from `acemq.jfrog.io`, you're licensed to run it.

This is the most common question from new customers, and it surprises people migrating from products that issue key files. Nothing to install, nothing to renew inside the broker.

## What your license unlocks technically

| | Community build | Licensed build |
|---|---|---|
| Newest available version | 3.13.7 | All supported streams — see [Version Support Matrix](/start-here/version-support-matrix) |
| Commercial plugins | — | Warm Standby Replication, Delayed Queues, Intra-cluster Compression, and others — see [Exclusive Features](/docs/features/availability-matrix) |
| Jump upgrades across versions | — | Supported |
| LTS support window | — | Through the stream's commercial support date |

See [What Is Tanzu?](/start-here/what-is-tanzu) for the full distinction.

## If your usage grows

<Callout kind="info">

**If your core usage exceeds your currently contracted amount, contact your AceMQ account owner.** Adding environments, scaling out a cluster, or standing up a new DR site can all move you past your contracted count. This is a commercial conversation, not a technical block — nothing stops you pulling and running the software, but your entitlement should reflect what you're actually running.

</Callout>

## Support scope

AceMQ support covers **all** RabbitMQ deployments — open-source, Bitnami, or Tanzu — under your agreement. Licensing adds the newer builds, LTS support, and commercial plugins above; it does not determine whether AceMQ will help you with what you're already running.

See [Getting Help](/support/getting-help) to open a ticket, and [Licensing FAQ](/support/licensing-faq) for common questions.
