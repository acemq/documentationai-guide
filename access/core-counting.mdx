---
title: How Licensing Is Counted
description: How AceMQ sizes a Tanzu RabbitMQ license — total CPU cores across every environment, including non-production.
---

Tanzu RabbitMQ licensing is sized on **total CPU cores**, not on node count, cluster count, or message volume.

## Every environment counts

<Callout kind="warning">

**All pre-production and production environments must be licensed.** Licensing production only, and leaving dev, test, staging, or DR unlicensed, is not permitted under Broadcom's licensing requirements. This is the most common sizing surprise for new customers.

</Callout>

When you total your cores, include:

- Production clusters
- Staging / pre-production
- Development and test
- Disaster recovery and warm standby clusters (see [Warm Standby Replication](/docs/features/warm-standby-replication))

## What AceMQ needs to quote

To size a license, AceMQ asks for:

1. **Total CPU core count** across all pre-production and production environments
2. **Whether those are physical or virtualized cores**
3. Which RabbitMQ version you're running or targeting
4. Roughly what the deployment looks like — node counts, clusters, and what the messaging layer carries

<Callout kind="alert">

**AceMQ Insight**

Customers very often supply a **vCPU** figure when asked for cores — for example "36 cores (72 vCPUs with hyperthreading)". These are not the same number, and which one applies materially changes your quote. State clearly which you're giving, and confirm the conversion with AceMQ before budgeting against it. Do not assume a 2:1 ratio applies to your environment.

</Callout>

## Minimum core commitments

Broadcom applies a minimum core commitment to Tanzu RabbitMQ licensing, and AceMQ's MSP relationship affects what minimum applies to you.

<Callout kind="warning">

**Confirm your applicable minimum with AceMQ before budgeting.** The minimum that applies depends on your product, region, and whether you're purchasing through AceMQ's MSP entitlement or another channel. Contact [AceMQ support](/support/getting-help) or your account contact for the figure that applies to your situation rather than relying on a number heard elsewhere.

</Callout>

## Regional availability

AceMQ's authorization to sell licensing varies by region and by the purchasing entity's location. If your deploying entity and your purchasing entity are in different countries, raise this early — it can affect whether AceMQ can supply the license directly or routes you to a regional partner.

## Counting cores you can't directly see

If you embed RabbitMQ in a product deployed behind your own customers' firewalls, exact core counts may not be observable. AceMQ works with customers in this position on a projection basis rather than requiring exact figures. Discuss your situation with your account contact — see [Getting Help](/support/getting-help).
