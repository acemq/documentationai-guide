## First login

Use the temporary password from your provisioning email at `acemq.jfrog.io`. You'll be prompted to set your own password on first login.

## Groups and permissions

Every user in your company is added to a company-specific JFrog group. That group needs **two permissions**, both required:

- **Read** — lets you browse and download
- **Deploy** — lets your tooling cache pulled images locally. **Without deploy, containerized builds (Docker, Helm) will fail**, even though read alone looks sufficient.

If you get a permissions error, see [Access Troubleshooting](/support/access-troubleshooting) — this is the most common cause.

## Service accounts

There's no separate "service account" provisioning path in JFrog. **A service account is just an invited user whose mailbox is a shared or team inbox** rather than an individual's. If you need CI/CD access, have your admin invite an address like `ci@yourcompany.com` through the normal [onboarding](/access/onboarding) flow.
