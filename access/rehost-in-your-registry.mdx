---
title: Re-host in Your Registry
description: Pull from AceMQ and push to your own registry — a supported pattern for teams that need images available inside their own network boundary.
---

Pulling from `acemq.jfrog.io` at deploy time and re-hosting in your own registry (Docker Hub, ECR, GCR, an internal registry) is a normal, supported pattern — several licensed customers do this so their Helm charts and CI/CD pipelines only ever reference internal registries.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pull from AceMQ" icon="download">

```bash
docker pull acemq.jfrog.io/rabbitmq-docker-remote/vmware-tanzu-rabbitmq:4.2.6
```

  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-tag for your registry" icon="tag">

```bash
docker tag acemq.jfrog.io/rabbitmq-docker-remote/vmware-tanzu-rabbitmq:4.2.6 \
  <your-registry>/vmware-tanzu-rabbitmq:4.2.6
```

  </Step>
  <Step title="Push and point your tooling at it" icon="upload">

```bash
docker push <your-registry>/vmware-tanzu-rabbitmq:4.2.6
```

Update your Helm values or manifests to reference `<your-registry>` instead of `acemq.jfrog.io`.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warning">

If you re-host, **pin by digest** (see [Pin by Digest](/access/pin-by-digest)) so your internal copy is guaranteed identical to what you pulled from AceMQ — a re-tag alone doesn't protect against your own registry's tag being overwritten later.

</Callout>
