This knowledge base is for one thing: **getting and staying licensed with AceMQ for Tanzu RabbitMQ.** How to get access, how to get your entitled packages, what your license covers, and how to migrate onto or between licensed formats.

<Callout kind="info">

This is not a RabbitMQ product manual. For broker configuration, exclusive-feature setup (Warm Standby Replication, Delayed Queues, OAuth 2.0, LDAP), or protocol questions, see [Broadcom TechDocs](https://techdocs.broadcom.com) or your AceMQ Premium support resources. This KB only covers what's specific to being an AceMQ *licensed* customer.

</Callout>

## Who this is for

Anyone at a licensed AceMQ customer who needs to:

- Get JFrog access provisioned and generate a pull token
- Download or pull an entitled Tanzu RabbitMQ package — OCI, RPM, OVA, Kubernetes, or Open Source
- Understand what a license covers, and for how long a version is supported
- Migrate from an unlicensed (Bitnami/OSS) deployment onto a licensed Tanzu build, or move between package formats
