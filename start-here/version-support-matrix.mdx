| **3.13** | Feb 2024 | 30 Sep 2024 | **31 Dec 2029** |

*End of Commercial Support dates are indicative, per Broadcom. Official commercial support lifecycle information can be found on the Broadcom support portal.*

## What this means for you

- **4.2 and 3.13 have the longest remaining commercial support** — 4.2 to mid-2030, 3.13 to the end of 2029. Both outlive 4.3.
- **4.0 commercial support ends 30 September 2026.** If that date has passed and you're still on 4.0, you're on an unsupported stream — contact [AceMQ support](/support/getting-help) and start your move now. See [Version Upgrades](/migrations/version-upgrades).
- This table shows *when a release stream stops being supported*. It does not mean every patch within a stream is equally current — see [Version & Support Policy](/support/version-policy) for the separate rule about which minor releases are actually licensed.
