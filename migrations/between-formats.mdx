---
title: Migrating Between Package Formats
description: Moving between Tanzu RabbitMQ delivery formats — for example VM-based OVA to Kubernetes, or RPM to containers — without changing your license.
---

Your AceMQ license isn't tied to a package format — it covers OCI, RPM, OVA, Kubernetes, and Open Source equally (see [What's Included](/start-here/whats-included)). If your infrastructure changes but your entitlement doesn't, moving formats is a deployment exercise, not a re-licensing one.

## Common moves

- **VM (OVA or RPM) → Kubernetes** — typically driven by wanting managed lifecycle (HA, reconciliation, scaling). See [Kubernetes](/packages/kubernetes).
- **RPM → containers (OCI)** — typically driven by containerizing existing infrastructure. See [OCI / Containers](/packages/oci).
- **Standalone OCI → Kubernetes** — typically driven by outgrowing a single-container or Compose deployment.

<Callout kind="info">

As with migrating from open source (see [From Open Source / Bitnami](/migrations/from-open-source)), treat a format change as **a new deployment with a data migration**, not an in-place conversion. There's no tooling that converts a running OVA instance into a Kubernetes-managed one.

</Callout>

## Approach

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pull the new format" icon="download">

Go to [Get Your Packages](/packages/oci) for your target format's quickstart.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Stand up in parallel" icon="copy">

Deploy the new format alongside your existing one. Match configuration values — they're broker-level settings, not format-specific.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Migrate data and cut over" icon="arrow-right-left">

Use RabbitMQ's own replication/federation tooling to move data, then cut traffic over.

  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Planning Checklist](/migrations/planning-checklist) before you start.
