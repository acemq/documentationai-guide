---
title: RPM 4.3 Supported Platforms
description: Supported operating systems for the Tanzu RabbitMQ 4.3 RPM.
---

Tanzu RabbitMQ RPMs are built and tested for:

- Red Hat Enterprise Linux 8 (RHEL 8)
- Red Hat Enterprise Linux 9 (RHEL 9)
- CentOS Stream 8 and 9
- Rocky Linux 8 and 9
- AlmaLinux 8 and 9
- Oracle Linux 8 and 9

These RPMs are compatible with any Enterprise Linux distribution based on EL8 or EL9.

<Callout kind="alert">

**AceMQ Insight**

EL10 support has not been consistently observed across all patch releases in AceMQ's repository — confirm availability for your specific target version before committing to it. If you're not on a supported EL-based distribution, use [OCI](/docs/oci/4.3/overview) or [OVA](/docs/ova/4.3/overview) instead.

</Callout>
