---
title: Kubernetes 4.2 Known Issues
description: Known issues and limitations for Tanzu RabbitMQ on Kubernetes 4.2.
---

- New required feature flags were introduced starting with 4.2.0 — review feature-flag status on any cluster being upgraded onto 4.2.
- The Standby Replication Operator was removed as of 4.2 — Warm Standby Replication moved to CRD-based configuration. If you're referencing older documentation that describes a separate operator, it doesn't apply to 4.2+.

<Callout kind="alert">

**AceMQ Insight**

Check feature flags before upgrading, not during the maintenance window — this is the step most likely to turn a planned upgrade into an incident.

</Callout>

For Erlang/OTP compatibility on 4.2, see [Version & Support Policy](/support/version-policy) — OTP 28 is only partially supported and only for brand-new clusters.
