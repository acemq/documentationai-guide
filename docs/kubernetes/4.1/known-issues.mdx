Same general upgrade cautions as other streams — review feature-flag status before upgrading onto or off of 4.1. See [Version & Support Policy](/support/version-policy) for Erlang/OTP compatibility and the current-minor licensing rule.

With 4.1's support window shorter than 4.2 or 3.13, AceMQ recommends planning a move to [4.2](/docs/kubernetes/4.2/overview) rather than investing further in 4.1-specific tooling.
